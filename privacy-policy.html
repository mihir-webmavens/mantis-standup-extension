Mantis Quick Standup · Chrome extension

Privacy Policy

Effective 25 September 2026 · Applies to version 1.0.1 and later

Mantis Quick Standup is an internal tool for the Webmavens team. It sends the standup you write to standup.webmavens.dev and nowhere else. It does not track you, store your data, or share it with anyone.

What the extension does

On a ticket page at projects.webmavens.dev/tickets/…, the extension adds a “+ Standup” button. When you write a planned action and click Add Standup, it creates a standup entry on standup.webmavens.dev for that ticket, using the login you already have on that site.

Data it reads and sends

The extension only handles data when you click Add Standup. At that point it uses:

DataWhere it comes fromWhere it goes
Ticket number and linkThe URL of the ticket page you are onstandup.webmavens.dev
Ticket priorityRead from the ticket page (Low if none is set)standup.webmavens.dev
Planned actionThe text you type into the extensionstandup.webmavens.dev
Form token and user IDThe standup site’s own “create standup” formSent back to standup.webmavens.dev with the standup

Requests to standup.webmavens.dev use your existing login session for that site. The extension never reads, copies or stores your password or cookies.

What it does not do

  • It does not store any data. Nothing is saved in the extension or on your computer.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • It does not send data to any server other than standup.webmavens.dev.
  • It does not run on any website other than projects.webmavens.dev.
  • It does not sell or share data with third parties.

Permissions

  • Access to projects.webmavens.dev: shows the Standup button on ticket pages and reads the ticket number and priority.
  • Access to standup.webmavens.dev: submits the standup to your account on the standup site.

Where your data is kept

Standups you submit are stored by standup.webmavens.dev, the same as standups you add on that site directly. That site’s own policies cover how long they are kept and who can see them.

Changes and questions
