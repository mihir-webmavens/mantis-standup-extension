Mantis Quick Standup · Chrome extension

Privacy Policy

Effective 28 September 2026 · Applies to version 1.7.0 and later

Mantis Quick Standup is an internal tool for the Webmavens team. It sends the standups and EOD updates you write to standup.webmavens.dev. If you set up the optional MantisAI chat, your messages and the ticket you are viewing go to Claude through the Claude CLI installed on your own computer. It does not track you or share your data with anyone else.

What the extension does

On a ticket page at projects.webmavens.dev/tickets/…, the extension adds a “+ Standup” button. When you write a planned action and click Add Standup, it creates a standup entry on standup.webmavens.dev for that ticket, using the login you already have on that site.

It also adds an “EOD” button, on every projects.webmavens.dev page, that lists today’s standups from the standup site’s EOD page, with a count. The same list is in the extension’s toolbar popup, so it works from any tab. You can edit the EOD update for any of them and click Save to update it on standup.webmavens.dev.

The extension’s toolbar icon shows how many EODs still have no update. If you turn on the optional standup reminder or EOD reminder, Chrome shows you a notification at the time you choose when you have not added a standup today, or when EODs are still empty.

To show the EOD button and list, it reads your EOD entries (ticket, planned action, priority, time and EOD update) from standup.webmavens.dev and shows them only inside the extension on your screen. When you save an EOD, the text you typed is sent to standup.webmavens.dev, together with that page’s form token and your other EOD entries exactly as the site already has them, so those stay unchanged.

For the toolbar count and the reminders, the extension also reads the same EOD page in the background about every 15 minutes and at reminder time. It only counts the entries; nothing is kept or sent anywhere else. Reminder notifications are shown by Chrome on your computer.

Requests to standup.webmavens.dev use your existing login session for that site. The extension never reads, copies or stores your password or cookies.

MantisAI (optional)

MantisAI is a chat panel on projects.webmavens.dev pages. It only works after you set it up yourself from the toolbar popup (Settings → MantisAI): you allow the optional native messaging permission and run an installer that adds a small helper program to your computer. The helper runs your own Claude CLI (Claude Code), signed in with your own Claude account; the extension has no AI service or API key of its own.

When you send a message, the extension passes to the helper on your computer:

  • the message you typed;
  • on a ticket page: the ticket number, address, title, priority and the page’s visible text (the ticket and its comments, up to about 40,000 characters), unless you switch off “ticket content included” above the message box;
  • on other pages: only the page address and title (Claude can still look things up in Mantis, as below);
  • the Claude model you picked in Settings, if any.

The Claude CLI sends this to Anthropic to produce the answer, under your Claude account and Anthropic’s terms and privacy policy, and keeps its own local history of the conversation (so it can be continued).

To answer questions about your work, Claude can look things up in Mantis through the Mantis connection (webmavens-projects) that you have signed in to in your Claude CLI: for example who you are, tickets, their notes and history, todos, project briefs and chat messages you can see. These lookups run as you, with your Mantis permissions, and what they return becomes part of the conversation sent to Anthropic. The panel lists each lookup.

Claude can only change something in Mantis (for example post a note, change a ticket’s status or send a chat message) after you click Approve on a card that shows exactly what will be done; if you click Deny, stop the reply or do not answer, nothing is changed. Claude has no other tools: it cannot run commands or read your files.

Your MantisAI conversations (your messages and the answers, one conversation per ticket, up to the 40 most recent) are saved in Chrome’s local extension storage on your computer so they are still there when you come back. New chat deletes the conversation for that ticket.

EOD from commits (optional, off until you turn it on in Settings) also uses the helper. At the time you choose, it reads your EOD list from standup.webmavens.dev; for EODs that are still empty, the helper runs git log in the repo folders you listed and returns only your own commits from that day (matched by each repo’s user.email). For each ticket with a commit that mentions its number, the ticket number, its planned action and those commit messages go to Claude through your Claude CLI, which writes the EOD; that Claude run has no tools and no Mantis connection. The EOD is then saved on standup.webmavens.dev like one you typed. The repo folder list is kept with your settings; the last run’s result stays in Chrome’s local storage on your computer.

What it does not do

  • It does not store your standups or EODs. It saves your settings (the button style and Mantis look you pick, the Kinetic playground switch and its game scores, your reminder choices, the Mantis header tabs you show and their order, and the MantisAI model), kept in Chrome’s extension storage and synced with your Chrome profile, and, if you use MantisAI, your conversations, kept only in Chrome’s local storage on your computer.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • The extension itself does not send data to any server other than standup.webmavens.dev. MantisAI messages go to the helper on your computer, which passes them to Anthropic through your Claude CLI; Mantis lookups go from your Claude CLI to projects.webmavens.dev.
  • It does not run on any website other than projects.webmavens.dev.
  • It does not sell or share data with third parties.

Permissions

  • Access to projects.webmavens.dev: shows the EOD button on its pages and the Standup button on ticket pages, and reads the ticket number and priority. It also reads the names of the Mantis header tabs (kept in Chrome’s local storage) and hides or reorders them as you choose in the Header tab. If you pick a Mantis look, it restyles these pages in your browser only; your last look is also kept in the site’s local storage so pages open in it without a flash.
  • Access to standup.webmavens.dev: submits standups, reads your EOD entries and saves EOD updates to your account on the standup site.
  • Storage: remembers the button style, Mantis look, reminder and header settings you choose from the extension’s toolbar icon.
  • Alarms: refreshes the pending-EOD count on the toolbar icon and schedules the reminders.
  • Notifications (optional): asked for only when you turn on a reminder; used to show the reminders.
  • Native messaging (optional): asked for only when you set up MantisAI; lets the extension talk to the MantisAI helper installed on your computer, and to nothing else.

Where your data is kept

Standups and EOD updates you submit are stored by standup.webmavens.dev, the same as standups you add on that site directly. That site’s own policies cover how long they are kept and who can see them.
